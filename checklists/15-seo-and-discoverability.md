# 15. SEO and discoverability (public sites)

**In plain English:** Help search engines and social platforms find and display your pages correctly.

**Applies when:** public website.

Priority: `P0` blocks release, `P1` fix before launch or within 30 days, `P2` backlog. See [scoring](../docs/scoring.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] **SEO-01** `P1` Crawlable, correct status codes, canonicals, sitemap, robots.txt; no accidental noindex
  - *Verify:* Search Console clean
- [ ] **SEO-02** `P1` Unique titles and descriptions; semantic headings; structured data validated
  - *Verify:* Rich results test
- [ ] **SEO-03** `P2` Open Graph and social cards; redirect map; broken-link monitoring; analytics with consent
  - *Verify:* Preview shares

## Standards and tools

- [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs): Official crawling, indexing and structured data guidance.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): Structured data vocabulary.
- [Open Graph protocol](https://ogp.me/): Social preview metadata.
- [Lighthouse](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse): Automated performance, accessibility and SEO audits.
