## Review checklist

- [ ] Product type, users, system boundary, deployment model, and data categories are documented.
- [ ] Conditional triggers are evaluated against actual architecture and features.
- [ ] N/A decisions include a reason and reassessment trigger.
- [ ] Legal, contractual, and sector-specific applicability questions are referred for qualified review when material.
- [ ] External framework editions and control sets are recorded.
- [ ] The matrix is revisited after material product, architecture, data, market, or threat changes.
